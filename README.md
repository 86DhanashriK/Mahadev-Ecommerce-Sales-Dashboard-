# 🛒 Mahadev Ecommerce Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Mahadev Ecommerce Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze ecommerce sales performance across different states, product categories, customers, payment methods, and time periods. The dashboard provides actionable insights into revenue, profit, quantity sold, and customer purchasing behavior to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🔍 Dashboard Features

### 🌍 Sales by State
- Analyzes sales performance across different states.
- Identifies top-performing and low-performing regions.

### 📦 Quantity by Category
- Displays product demand across categories:
  - Clothing
  - Electronics
  - Furniture

### 📈 Monthly Profit Analysis
- Tracks profit trends throughout the year.
- Highlights profitable and loss-making months.

### 👥 Sales by Customer
- Identifies top customers contributing to overall sales.
- Helps understand customer value and purchasing behavior.

### 💳 Quantity by Payment Mode
- Analyzes customer payment preferences:
  - Cash on Delivery (COD)
  - UPI
  - Debit Card
  - Credit Card
  - EMI

### 🏷 Profit by Sub-Category
- Evaluates profitability across product sub-categories such as:
  - Printers
  - Bookcases
  - Sarees
  - Accessories
  - Tables

### 🎛 Interactive Filters
Users can dynamically filter data by:
- Quarter (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)
- State

## 💡 Key Insights

✔ Clothing contributes the highest share of total quantity sold.

✔ Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most preferred payment method.

✔ Maharashtra generates the highest sales revenue.

✔ Printers are the most profitable product sub-category.

✔ Profitability fluctuates throughout the year, with strong performance in January and November.
